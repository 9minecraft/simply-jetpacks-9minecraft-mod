# Simply Jetpacks — Hover to Build High Structures

Simply Jetpacks 9minecraft Mod adds wearable machines that let you fly using stored energy. You can strap one of these jetpacks to your chest and take off into the sky whenever you want. It gives you full control over how you move through the air, making building high structures or escaping danger much easier. The pack includes different options for speed, battery life, and protection, giving you a reason to upgrade your gear as you play.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Thomas Wolfgruber**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
